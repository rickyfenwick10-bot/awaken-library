# Awaken Open Library

Free survival, first aid and preparedness documents to use with the **Awaken** offline survival app (Android). Everything here is meant to be downloaded and kept on your own phone, tablet or computer, so it works with no internet.

**These are guides, not a replacement for first aid training or the emergency services. In an emergency call 999 or 112.**

## What is in each folder

Open a folder to read or download a single document. To get everything: green Code button, then Download ZIP (about 370 MB). Keep it on your phone, tablet or a data pen so it works offline.

## Notes
- Many documents are written for US or military use. Numbers, products and procedures may not match UK or European practice. Read the guide, then apply it to where you are.
- Some PDFs carry a "Free PDF hosted by trueprepper.com" cover page from the place they were originally obtained. The underlying documents are US Government or public domain works.
